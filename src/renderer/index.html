How Recaller works

Each project is a column. Inside it, prompts move down through three stages: cooking, cooked, served.

  1. CookingNew prompts: type one (New text) or speak one (Dictate). Click any card to copy it.
  2. CookedHover a prompt and click ↓ Cooked (or drag it down) and they join into one note.
  3. ServedMove the Cooked note down (button, swipe left or drag) and it's added to the end of the final text.

Handy moves

  • Copy several at once: hold Ctrl and click cards. They're copied in click order.
  • Delete: hold a card and swipe it left. In Cooked or Served, open Notes and click the trash on one note, or right-click the text and use the editor's trash to delete all of it. Ctrl Z undoes anything.
  • Edit anything: right-click a prompt, the Cooked note or the Served text (Cooked and Served also have a pencil). In Notes, right-click one part to edit just that part, or reorder, send back or delete it.
  • Fold a stage away: click its name (Cooking, Cooked or Served).
  • Eating, then Eaten: once you've handed the Served text to an agent, switch on Eating in Served. The board's bottom right shows every column that's eating. When the agent is done, click ✓ there (or Eating › Done): the prompt moves to Eaten (the fork and knife in the top bar) and Served is ready for the next batch. Eaten keeps every finished prompt by day, to copy, search or serve again.
  • Claude Code: the >_ button under the Cooked note wraps its prompts in a Claude Code task (request, how to work, what to report). Click it again for the original. Moving it to Served serves the formatted text.
  • Tidy the board: a column's ⋯ menu can minimize it to a slim strip (click the strip to open it), or put it in a group such as “Important”. Click a group's name to fold all its columns away.
  • Highlight a prompt to check it again later: hover, then click the marker.
  • Resize a column: drag its right edge. The ↔ button in the top bar resets every width.
  • Screenshots: the camera in the editor, or paste an image. They follow the prompt into Cooked and Served, shown under the text: click the text, paste, then click the picture and paste again.
  • On every computer: Settings › Account & sync keeps this board in your account. Column widths, folds, drafts and the look stay on each computer.
